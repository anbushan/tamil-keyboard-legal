# Tamil Keyboard — Privacy policy

_Last updated 7 November 2026_

## In short

What you type is processed on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else. We have no servers and no accounts. The keyboard itself shows no ads. The only typed or spoken words that can leave your phone are voice typing handled by your phone’s speech service. The app counts which screens and features are used (never what you type); you can turn this off.

## What you type

Tanglish-to-Tamil conversion, word suggestions, gesture typing and emoji suggestions all run on your phone.

To improve suggestions, the keyboard remembers words you pick and which words follow each other. This is stored only on your phone. You can see and delete it in “My words”, or turn learning off in Typing settings.

In password fields and fields marked private, the keyboard does not learn, does not save clipboard history and does not show suggestions.

## Clipboard history

Text you copy is kept in a clipboard history on your phone (up to 40 items, plus any you pin). Items Android marks as sensitive, such as copied passwords, are skipped. You can clear it in Typing settings → Privacy.

## Text shortcuts

Shortcuts you create are stored only on your phone.

## Voice typing

When you open the 🎤 panel, the keyboard uses your phone’s speech recognition service (usually Google’s) and the microphone permission. That service may process your voice on its servers unless you have installed offline speech recognition for Tamil. We do not receive or keep any audio. The microphone is used only while the panel is open.

## Handwriting and photo stickers

Handwriting recognition and background removal for photo stickers use Google ML Kit. The first time, the Tamil handwriting model (about 20 MB) and the background-removal module are downloaded from Google. After that, recognition and background removal run on your phone; your drawings and photos are not uploaded.

## Translate

Translation between Tamil and English uses Google ML Kit on your phone. The first time, the language pack (about 30 MB) is downloaded from Google. After that, translation runs on your phone and your text is not uploaded.

## Chat stickers

If you allow it, the app reads the sticker folder you choose, only to show those stickers to you. Stickers you import are copied into the keyboard’s own sticker collection. Nothing is uploaded. You can remove access at any time in Android settings.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows one note a morning: a Thirukkural, a proverb, a Tanglish word or a keyboard tip. These notes are scheduled on your phone and nothing is sent to or from a server to show them. You can turn them off in Typing settings or in Android settings.

## Backup

If you choose “Back up to a file”, your settings, learned words and shortcuts are saved to a file in the place you pick. Clipboard history is not included. We do not receive this file; keep it somewhere you trust, because your learned words are in it.

## Opening apps from the keyboard

To suggest apps while you type and to show the ⊞ Apps panel, the keyboard reads the list of apps on your phone that have a home-screen icon. This list never leaves your phone. You can turn app suggestions off in Typing settings.

## Usage statistics

The Tamil Keyboard app (not the keyboard) uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count which screens are opened and which features are used, for example “a theme was chosen” or “a sticker was made”. It never sends anything you type, your words, shortcuts, photos or file names. Google receives an app-instance identifier and basic device information (model, Android version, country). The advertising ID is not collected. You can turn this off any time in Typing settings → Privacy → Share usage statistics. See https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Ratings

The keyboard counts how many Tamil words you type and stickers you send (only the numbers, never the text) so the app can ask for a Google Play rating at a good moment. These counts stay on your phone.

## Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Delete learned words in “My words”, clear clipboard history in Typing settings, or uninstall the app to remove all of it.

## Children

Tamil Keyboard is not directed at children under 13.

## Android’s keyboard warning

When you turn on any keyboard you install, Android shows a standard warning that the keyboard could collect what you type. Tamil Keyboard does not send what you type anywhere.
